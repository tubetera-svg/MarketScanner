# Strategy Information

This file contains the exact conditions used by strategy runners in `all_strategy.py`.

## Strategies

### `points_of_interest`
Summary: Information only: the nearest point of interest (FVG, untaken swing or CISD level) between the active protected swing and current price. No trade plan.
- Uses the latest protected-swing analysis without creating a trade plan.
- Reports the triggered protected level and type: `fvg`, `sweep`, or `CISD`.
- Selection is point-in-time from the active protected low/high (sweep extreme) toward current price: FVG first (reported at its near edge; gaps narrower than 0.1 x ATR(14) are skipped), then an untaken swing/liquidity level, then a CISD (open of the first candle of an opposing series, later closed through).
- Note shows the POI, the protected low/high with the date it was made, and the CISD confirmation level.
- Output contains direction, triggered level, type, state, and note; entry, stop-loss, target, and R:R are intentionally omitted.
- POI rows are informational and do not create backtest positions.

### `candle_3_closure`
Summary: Information only: a Candle 2 or Candle 3 closure (TTrades fractal model) at a protected-swing point of interest. Arms an intraday confirmation for the next session.
- Detects Candle 2 and Candle 3 closures (TTrades fractal model) at a protected-swing POI; `closure_type` says which.
- POI (as known before the reaction candles): the FVG -> swing -> CISD point of interest worked from the protected swing, then the protected (CISD) level itself.
- Candle 2 closure (bullish; bearish mirrors): the latest candle reaches the POI, sweeps the previous candle's low and closes back above it.
- Candle 3 closure: Candle 2 reached the POI, did NOT sweep Candle 1's low, and closed down; Candle 3 closes over Candle 2's body.
- Reports the closure candle's high, low, equilibrium, POI level/type and direction; arms an intraday confirmation for the next session in the Candle 4 half (upper half bullish / lower half bearish).
- Informational only: it does not emit entry, stop-loss, target, R:R, or backtest positions.

### `propulsion_blocks`
Summary: An order-block displacement, a pullback into the block, then a confirming displacement. The propulsion candle's open is the entry reference and its midpoint (mean threshold) is the stop.
Point-in-time propulsion blocks: an order-block displacement, retracement into
the block, opposite candle, and confirming displacement. The propulsion candle
open is the entry reference (`entry`; the backtest still fills at the next
open) and its mean threshold is the invalidation/stop level. The mean
threshold is the midpoint of the propulsion candle's full range or of its body
(Settings -> Strategy parameters). A close through the mean invalidates the
block whether it lands before or after confirmation, and a block price closed
through before the retrace is discarded. Targets use prior-week / pre-week
swing liquidity (2R measured-move fallback). A confirmation arms an intraday
confirmation for the next session (zone = propulsion open to mean).

### `inside_bar_pattern_daily_sweep`
Summary: A strong-bodied day, then an inside day, then today sweeps one side of the inside day and closes back inside it: bullish when the low is swept, bearish when the high is swept.
- Uses the latest 3 daily candles.
- Bullish condition `_inside_bar_bullish()` requires:
  - prior day 2 is bullish (`d2_open` < `d2_close`)
  - prior day 2 body size > 60% of its full range
  - current day 1 is fully inside day 2 range (`d1_high` <= `d2_high`, `d1_low` >= `d2_low`)
  - current day low is below day 1 low
  - current day high is below day 1 high
  - current close is above day 1 low
- Bearish condition `_inside_bar_bearish()` requires:
  - prior day 2 is bearish (`d2_open` > `d2_close`)
  - prior day 2 body size > 60% of its full range
  - day 1 is inside day 2 range (`d1_high` < `d2_high`, `d1_low` > `d2_low`)
  - current day high is above day 1 high
  - current day low is above day 1 low
  - current close is below day 1 high
- Output: bullish or bearish inside-bar breakout matches.

### `ema5_sweep`
Summary: Two days held beyond the 5-day EMA, and today runs the previous day's high (bullish) or low (bearish) but closes back inside its range.
- Uses the latest 6+ daily candles and computes a 5-period EMA on close prices.
- Bullish condition `_ema5_sweep_bullish()` requires:
  - current low > current EMA5
  - previous low > previous EMA5
  - current high > previous high
  - current close < previous high
- Bearish condition `_ema5_sweep_bearish()` requires:
  - current high < current EMA5
  - previous high < previous EMA5
  - current low < previous low
  - current close > previous low
- Output: daily EMA momentum bias signals.

## Weekly Profile Strategies (6-profile weekly series)

Six additional strategies in `all_strategy.py` model the weekly profile framework (Classic Expansion, Midweek Reversal, Consolidation Reversal, Intraweek Reversal, Thursday Counter, TGIF Setup). All of them:

- Use only end-of-day NSE bhavcopy daily candles for the current calendar week (Mon=0..Fri=4 weekday tagging), so a mid-week scan evaluates the week in progress and a Friday/weekend anchor evaluates the completed week. "Made on Mon/Tue" style checks use the weekday, so a holiday cannot shift the days.
- Fire once per week: `final_signal` is True only on the first session a profile matches (earlier sessions of the week are re-evaluated point-in-time); later matching sessions stay listed with `note` "first signalled <date>".
- A first match at Friday's close is a label only (`state=expired`, `friday_close_label_only`): the weekly profile has played out and the next entry would fall in a different week.
- Approximate the videos' hourly "change in the state of delivery" confirmation with daily candle-two closures: a strong directional close (body >= 50% of the day range) through the prior day's range. When the intraday confirmation watcher is on, a fired profile also arms an hourly/15m CISD confirmation for the rest of the week (see "Intraday confirmation").
- Share lookback 60 calendar days (`WEEKLY_PROFILE_LOOKBACK_DAYS`) to cover the pre-week swing/prior-week objective references.
- Emit result columns `symbol`, `bullish_match`, `bearish_match`, `final_signal`, `status`, plus `profile` (human-readable label) and `note` (phase/detail string), keeping outputs clubbed per strategy in bull/bear/all files and in `main.py`'s combined `all_strategy_matches_*.csv`.

### Flags
- `WEEKLY_PROFILES_ENABLED = True` — master switch; when False none of the six appear in `strategy_registry()` and nothing runs.
- `WEEKLY_PROFILE_FLAGS` — per-strategy dict (`classic_expansion_sweep`, `midweek_reversal_sweep`, `consolidation_reversal_sweep`, `intraweek_reversal_sweep`, `thursday_counter_sweep`, `tgif_setup_sweep`); set any entry False to exclude that single profile.

### Web UI / API selection layer
- The "Strategy profiles" panel in the web app shows every strategy as a toggle chip (Core group + Weekly profiles group). Clicking a chip flips that strategy ON/OFF; the choice is persisted in `config/strategy_flags.json`. Settings -> Scanning has the same Run toggles plus Show (hide from the scanner page).
- Endpoints served by `api/main.py` via `api/strategy_bridge.py`: `GET /api/strategies` (catalog + master-switch state), `PUT /api/strategies/{name}` (`{"enabled": true|false}`), `POST /api/strategy-scan` (`{"symbols": [...], "strategies": [...]?, "anchor_date": "YYYY-MM-DD"?}` — defaults to all enabled strategies over the selected watchlist symbols).
- `POST /api/strategy-scan` returns results clubbed per strategy (`results[].bullish` / `results[].bearish` rows carry symbol, profile label, note, TradingView link).
- Code-level flags remain hard gates: a weekly profile chip cannot be turned ON from the UI while `WEEKLY_PROFILES_ENABLED` or its `WEEKLY_PROFILE_FLAGS` entry is False in `all_strategy.py`.

### Tunables
- `CLASSIC_EXPANSION_MAX_EXPANSION_DAYS = 3` — stops looking for continuation after 2-3 expansion days.
- `CONSOLIDATION_MAX_RANGE_PCT = 0.05` / `CONSOLIDATION_MAX_DRIFT_PCT = 0.02` — Mon-Wed range width and net-drift caps defining a genuine consolidation.
- `INTRAWEEK_STALL_BODY_RATIO = 0.4` — Tuesday indecision body threshold.
- `THURSDAY_COUNTER_BIAS_MIN_PCT = 0.01` — minimum Mon-open-to-Wed-close net move establishing the weekly bias.
- `TGIF_EXPANSION_MIN_PCT = 0.01` — minimum expansion leg size off the weekly extreme before Friday may be faded.

### `classic_expansion_sweep`
Summary: The week's low (or high) forms on Monday/Tuesday and price expands away from it for up to three days with strong closes.
- Bullish requires: week's lowest low made Mon/Tue and never broken after; 1-3 post-extreme sessions (stops after 2-3 expansion days); latest close displaced beyond the first post-extreme open; latest session is a candle-two closure up (close > prior high, bullish body >= 50%) or, on Friday, an upward close vs the prior close (slowing tolerated — reported as a label only, never a trade, since Friday is not an expansion day).
- Bearish is the mirror image with the week's highest high made Mon/Tue.
- Notes report e.g. `extreme_d1_expand2_candle_two_closure`; rejections explain (`weekly_extreme_late_d3`, `expansion_days=4`, `candle_two_pending`, ...).

### `midweek_reversal_sweep`
Summary: Monday and Tuesday push one way, then Wednesday (or Thursday) reverses with a close through the prior day; later days must hold the reversal.
- Bullish requires: early-week decline (Tuesday closes below Monday's close and takes out Monday's low); Wednesday pivot reversal closure above Tuesday's high with a bullish close; every later session holds above the pivot close. Matched already on Wednesday evening as `pivot_wed_await_continuation`.
- Thursday fallback (per the source): when Wednesday lacks a clean closure (`wednesday_unclear_await_thursday`), a Thursday closure above Wednesday's high with a bullish close is the pivot (`pivot_thu_fallback_*`). SL uses the Wed..pivot extreme.
- Bearish mirrors (early rally, pivot closes below the prior day's low).

### `consolidation_reversal_sweep`
Summary: Monday to Wednesday stay in a tight range; Thursday fakes out one side and closes back inside. Target is the opposite side of the range.
- Mon-Wed must be a genuine consolidation: range width <= 5% of Wednesday's close AND net drift from Monday's open <= 2%.
- Thursday must fake one side: trades beyond the consolidation high but closes back inside (bearish expectation), or below the low closing back inside (bullish expectation). Aggressive entry is signalled immediately on Thursday (`thursday_fake_break_aggressive_entry`).
- If Friday exists it must expand opposite the fake break (close beyond Thursday's close) for `friday_expansion_confirmed`; a close back outside the range invalidates (`friday_invalidated`).
- Target: the opposite side of the Mon-Wed consolidation range (source target).

### `intraweek_reversal_sweep`
Summary: Monday expands one way, Tuesday stalls, then Wednesday or Thursday closes strongly the other way (Candle 2 or Candle 3 closure).
- Bullish requires: Monday directional expansion down (bearish body >= 50%); Tuesday stall (inside bar or body <= 40%); then a reversal closure on Wednesday or Thursday - Thursday fallback is allowed when Wednesday is not clean. Either a Candle 2 closure (close beyond the prior day's high, bullish close, body >= 50%) or a Candle 3 closure (the prior day closed down without sweeping the day before it, and the pivot closes over its body) qualifies; the note says which (`candle_two_closure_dN` / `candle_three_closure_dN`).
- Later sessions must hold the reversal structure (no close beyond the pivot day's opposite extreme).
- Bearish mirrors (Monday expansion up, stall, downside reversal closure).

### `thursday_counter_sweep`
Summary: After a clear trend Monday to Wednesday, Thursday takes out Wednesday's extreme and closes back against it. Target is the weekly open.
- Requires an established directional week by Wednesday: |Wednesday close - Monday open| >= 1% of price, with Wednesday extending the extreme (high for up-weeks, low for down-weeks).
- Thursday must sweep liquidity against nothing - i.e. in an up-week take out Wednesday's high then fail, closing below Wednesday's close (bearish counter); mirror for down-weeks (bullish counter).
- Signal fires Thursday evening (`thursday_counter_set_friday_pending`); Friday confirms continuation (`friday_continuation_confirmed`) or fails it (`friday_continuation_failed`).
- Target: the weekly open (Monday's open), the source's most common target.

### `tgif_setup_sweep`
Summary: The week's extreme formed Mon/Tue and the expansion reached a higher-timeframe objective by Wed/Thu; fades Friday back into the weekly range (25% retrace target).
- Critical rule enforced: no expansion + no HTF objective reached = no TGIF signal.
- Requires: weekly extreme formed Mon/Tue; expansion leg completing Wed or Thu with move >= 1% off the weekly extreme; objective actually reached — prior week high/low, the 10-session pre-week extreme, or a swing high/low (equal highs/lows included) or FVG edge from the 20 sessions before the week (`TGIF_OBJECTIVE_SESSIONS`), counted only when it lies beyond the weekly open; then Friday retracing into the weekly range without new extremes (Friday fade signal - bearish after bullish weeks, bullish after bearish weeks).
- Scenario handling: if scanned on Thursday with no Friday bar, "Thursday already reversed" variant matches when Thursday closes against the leg without exceeding its extreme (`thursday_reversed_friday_continuation`). This is the only tradeable variant on daily data; the Friday-close stage is a label (see "Fire once per week" above) and is traded intraday by the confirmation watcher instead.
- Target: 25% retracement of the weekly range (`TGIF_RETRACE_TARGET_PCT`, midpoint of the source's 20-30%).

### Multi-timeframe bias (extra info, not a strategy)
- Shown on every strategy row as `ctx_bias_d/w/m` (+ `ctx_bias_confluence`, `ctx_bias_direction`) when "Extra info" is on; see `src/daily_context.py` `multi_timeframe_bias`. Never gates a signal.
- Same rule on each timeframe, closed bars only: bullish if the bar closes above the previous bar's high, or sweeps its low and closes back above it (bearish mirrored; outside bar closing inside = Neutral). An unfinished week/month is skipped (mid-week reads W-1 vs W-2).

### `daily_bias_invalidation`
Summary: Reads the daily bias from the last closed candle, then signals when it is invalidated (opposite sweep or a close through the candle's midpoint) and the next day continues the other way.
- Establishes the daily bias from completed historical daily candles only.
- Bullish bias: the reference close exceeds the prior day's high, or the reference candle sweeps the prior low and closes back inside (a candle-2 closure); bearish is the mirror; conflicting reads are Neutral.
- Invalidation (only two things count, per the source): an opposing setup — the next candle sweeps the reference candle's bias-side extreme and closes back inside it (an opposite candle 2) — or EQ disrespect — the next candle closes through the reference candle's EQ.
- The following candle must continue the other way (close beyond the invalidation close) without reclaiming EQ.
- Bullish bias invalidation produces only bearish continuation signals; bearish bias invalidation produces only bullish continuation signals.
- Target is the reference candle's opposite extreme; when price is already beyond it, target and R:R are left empty.
- Wicks, consolidation, drift, and EQ breaches without opposite continuation are ignored. A signal arms an intraday confirmation for the next session.
- The runner uses only data available through `as_of_date`, so live scans must provide closed bars and backtests cannot use future data.

### `protected_swings`
Summary: Finds the swing low/high the trend should hold (after a liquidity sweep or an FVG tap) and signals on the day a close confirms it; trades in that bias direction.
- Self-contained strategy module (`src/protected_swings.py`) implementing ICT-style **protected swings**: a swing high/low that is expected to hold while the trend continues. Detection primitives are discrete and unit-tested in `tests/test_protected_swings.py`; the thin `run_protected_swings` runner in `all_strategy.py` plugs them into the standard `StrategyExecution` contract.
- **Swings**: fractal detection (`detect_swing_points`, `SWING_LEFT_BARS=SWING_RIGHT_BARS=2`) identifies candidate short-term swing highs/lows.
- **FVG**: `find_fvgs` scans the recent window for 3-candle fair value gaps using the *same definition* as `ict_scanner.detect_bullish_fvg`/`detect_bearish_fvg` (`c1.high < c3.low` bullish with a bullish c2; `c1.low > c3.high` bearish with a bearish c2).
- **Sweep-based protected swing**: bullish — price runs below a recent swing low (liquidity sweep via `detect_liquidity_sweep`), then a candle *closes above the open* of the down-close series that delivered price into the sweep; bearish is the mirror. The series ends at the sweep candle when it closes in the series direction, otherwise (e.g. a hammer) at the candle before it — so a green sweep candle can itself confirm. Confirmation is CISD-style (open), not the wick extreme.
- **FVG-based protected swing**: price trades into an FVG zone (a low reaches `gap_high` for bullish / a high reaches `gap_low` for bearish), then a candle closes beyond the open of the same-direction series immediately before that entry.
- **Protected (CISD) level**: the open of the *first* candle of the protecting series (TTrades CISD definition). Confirmed when a close breaches it.
- **Protected low/high (`sweep_extreme`)**: the extreme made from the sweep (or FVG series) through confirmation. A later close beyond it invalidates the swing; the stop is placed beyond it (+/- 1 ATR). Targets use prior-week / pre-week swing liquidity (2R measured-move fallback).
- A confirmation arms an intraday confirmation for the next session (Candle 4 half of the confirmation candle, invalidation = the protected low/high).
- **Formation tag**: every candidate carries `tag` = `sweep_based` or `fvg_based` (the pathway that produced it); surfaced in results and the UI chip (hover shows full level detail).
- **Single-day event**: a swing is reported (and a trade plan produced) only on the session it is *confirmed*; a swing that merely persists into later sessions is not re-listed, so the same pattern does not reappear in scan results or re-fire a backtest trade every day. Anticipated (unconfirmed) swings are reported for awareness but never fire a signal.
- **State machine per swing** (point-in-time, re-derived from the series truncated to `as_of_date`): `anticipated` (swept/traded-in but not yet confirmed) -> `confirmed` (qualifying close landed) -> `invalidated` (price later closes back beyond the protected low/high). A swing is only a *signal* once `confirmed`.
- **Trend / bias**: the most recent confirmed, non-invalidated protected swing sets the live bias — protected low -> bullish (buys), protected high -> bearish (sells). Each newer confirmed swing in the bias direction becomes the new active "stepping-stone" protected level; an opposite-direction swing flips the bias.
- **Output**: one result row per symbol with `bullish_match`/`bearish_match`/`final_signal` (True only on the confirmation session), plus `profile`, `note`, `state`, `direction`, `entry`, `sl`/`target`/`rr`, `atr`, `track_mode`, `swing_level`, `protected_level` and `tag`. `entry`/`sl`/`target` reuse the project's standard `_build_trade_plan` (entry = current close, SL = swept swing extreme +/- 1x ATR buffer, target = measured move).
- **No signal when anticipated**: an anticipated swing populates `state=anticipated` and the detail row but sets `final_signal=False`, so the backtest engine never opens a trade on an unconfirmed close (no look-ahead / repainting).
- **Lookahead safety**: every primitive only ever reads indices <= the last available bar; the backtest engine slices the `daily_map` to `as_of_date` before calling the runner.
- **Lookback**: `PROTECTED_SWINGS_LOOKBACK_DAYS = 80` (registered in `run_strategies`'s `lookback_by_strategy`).

## Intraday confirmation (LTF)
- The TTrades frameworks enter only after an intraday change in the state of delivery (CISD). Daily bars can only show that after the move, so fired signals from the weekly profiles, `daily_bias_invalidation`, `candle_3_closure`, `protected_swings` and `propulsion_blocks` also write `ltf_zone_low/high`, `ltf_invalidation`, `ltf_signal_date` and `ltf_valid_until` (next session; rest of the week for weekly profiles).
- The watcher (Settings -> Intraday confirmation watcher; `GET /api/ltf-confirmation`, `POST /api/ltf-confirmation/check`) arms these once each market's daily bar is final, then replays completed TradingView bars (1h or 15m — Settings -> Strategy parameters) for armed symbols only through `src/ltf_confirmation.evaluate_ltf`: price must trade into the zone, then close beyond the open of the first candle of the latest opposite-colour series. A close through the invalidation first invalidates; the window running out expires it. Stop = the intraday extreme made in the zone. State persists in `data/state/ltf_setups.json`.
- Backtests still run on daily bars only (daily proxy); no intraday history is stored yet.

## Daily context columns (display only)
- `run_strategies` appends `ctx_*` columns (src/daily_context.py) to every strategy's rows; they never gate or change a signal. Computed from the same completed daily bars the strategies use (NSE in-progress bar trimmed; stale symbols skipped).
- `ctx_adr` / `ctx_adr_used_pct`: mean High-Low of the 20 bars before the signal bar, and the signal bar's range as % of it (ADR exhaustion).
- `ctx_opposing_wick_pct` / `ctx_wick_class`: lower wick (bullish rows) or upper wick (bearish rows) as % of range; small <= 25%, large >= 50%.
- `ctx_candle_type` / `ctx_next_day_bias`: signal bar vs previous bar - inside, outside, close_above_pdh / close_below_pdl (continuation), swept_pdh_rejected / swept_pdl_rejected (reversal). `ctx_cont_streak` counts consecutive continuation closures (+up/-down); `ctx_phase_change` is True at 3+.
- `ctx_prev_wick_mid` / `ctx_prev_wick_status`: if the previous bar swept and rejected, the midpoint of its wick and whether the signal bar respected or closed through it. `ctx_prev_eq` / `ctx_prev_eq_status`: previous bar's midpoint and whether the signal bar held its upper/lower half.
- `ctx_pdh/pdl`, `ctx_pwh/pwl`, `ctx_pmh/pml`: previous day / completed week / completed month extremes (week/month left empty if history starts inside that period). `ctx_draw_above/below`: nearest unswept 3-bar swing high above / low below the close (60-bar window).

## Data guard
- Every runner marks a symbol `status=stale` (no signal) when its latest daily bar is older than the session expected for `as_of_date` in that market (NSE calendar strictly; TradingView tolerates one missing session), so a suspended or unsynced symbol is not reported as current.

## ICT Scanner (`ict_scanner.py`)

### Overview
- Adaptive scanning framework implementing ICT (Inner Circle Trader) concepts for market structure analysis
- Implements Section 19 (Adaptive Scanning Frequency), Section 20 (Stock Scanning Priority), and Section 21 (NSE Market-Day Rule)
- Supports multiple market sessions: NSE (9:15 AM - 3:30 PM IST) and Forex 24x5 (Sunday 5:00 PM ET - Friday 5:00 PM ET)
- Loads watchlist from file with auto-detected or manually specified session types

### Adaptive Scanning States (Section 19)
State machine with dynamic check intervals based on market structure proximity:
- **FAR_FROM_POI** (Point of Interest): 15-minute check intervals for stocks far from key levels
- **APPROACHING_POI**: 5-minute check intervals as price nears significant price levels
- **LIQUIDITY_EVENT**: 1-minute check intervals during active liquidity sweeps
- **CONFIRMED_TRADE**: 1-5 minute intervals (volatility dependent) after entry confirmation
- **INVALIDATED**: 15-minute intervals when trade setup is invalidated, reverting to structure monitoring

### Stock Scanning Priority Tiers (Section 20)
Four-tier classification system for monitoring priority:
- **Tier A** (High Priority): Near HTF (Higher Time Frame) POI + clear Direction of Liquidity (DOL) + strong daily bias → 5-minute active monitoring
- **Tier B** (Medium Priority): Clear daily bias but distant from POI → 15-minute monitoring
- **Tier C** (Low Priority): No clear bias or conflicting structure → not actively monitored
- **Tier D** (Ignored): No meaningful liquidity or POI → entirely ignored

Tier classification is computed based on four factors:
- `near_poi`: proximity to higher timeframe points of interest
- `has_dol`: presence of defined direction of liquidity
- `strong_bias`: strength of daily price action bias
- `clear_bias`: clarity of directional bias

### NSE Market-Day Rule (Section 21)
- Ensures fresh daily candle analysis at NSE market boundaries (no overnight bleed)
- Triggers full re-scan once per trading day at market open (9:15 AM IST)
- Detects weekends and market holidays automatically
- Prevents false signals from after-market spillover data

### Watchlist Format
- Plain text file (e.g., `watchlist.txt`) with one symbol per line
- Supports auto-detection via broker prefixes: `OANDA:`, `FOREXCOM:`, `CAPITALCOM:`, `FXCM:`, `PEPPERSTONE:`, `SAXO:`, etc. for Forex
- NSE symbols default to NSE session unless prefixed with `NSE:` or suffixed with `|nse`
- Forex symbols can be explicitly marked with `|forex` suffix
- Example formats:
  - `RELIANCE` → NSE session (default)
  - `OANDA:XAUUSD` → Forex 24x5 (auto-detected)
  - `NSE:RELIANCE` → NSE session (explicit)

## Environment Variables
All optional: an unset variable means the default shown. Set them in the host's environment (Render dashboard → Environment; locally `setx NAME "value"` then open a new terminal / relaunch the scanner). Secrets (tokens, keys) live only in the environment, never in the repo or `config/*.json`. Booleans accept 1/0/true/false/yes/no/on.

### Data sources & sync (`market_data/config.py`)
- `FETCH_TRADINGVIEW_DATA` (default true): allow TradingView fetches (forex, commodities, crypto, TV-sourced symbols)
- `FETCH_NSE_DATA` (default true): allow NSE bhavcopy fetches; also gates the NSE holiday-list refresh
- `AUTO_FETCH_MISSING_DATA` (default true): allow automatic backfill of missing dates
- `BACKDATE_LOOKBACK_DAYS` (default 14, clamped 1–120): calendar days fetched around a backdate/historical test
- `TRADINGVIEW_DEFAULT_EXCHANGE` (default `NSE`): exchange used for TradingView symbols without a prefix

### Storage & state
- `MARKET_DATA_DB_PATH` (default `data/market_data.db`): SQLite file location; point it at persistent storage on a host
- `APP_STATE_STORE` (default unset = files): `db` keeps settings, watchlist, favorites, alerts, auth and automation state in the DB (`app_state`) instead of `config/` / `data/state/` files; needed on hosts with a wiped disk
- `MARKET_SCANNER_SETTINGS_PATH` (default `config/app_settings.json`): alternative settings file
- `TURSO_DATABASE_URL` (default unset = local SQLite): `libsql://…` URL; when set, the app runs on Turso instead of the local DB. Set only on the host, never on the local PC
- `TURSO_AUTH_TOKEN`: Turso token for `TURSO_DATABASE_URL` (secret)
- `TURSO_SYNC_URL` / `TURSO_SYNC_TOKEN` (local PC only): enable "Sync to online (Turso)" on the Database page and `python -m market_data.turso_sync`; the local app keeps using local SQLite

### API & frontend
- `CORS_ORIGINS` (default: local dev origins only): comma-separated extra allowed origins, e.g. the Vercel frontend URL
- `NEXT_PUBLIC_API_URL` (frontend; default `http://127.0.0.1:8000`): API base URL. Baked in at `next build`, so rebuild after changing it

### Push notifications (`api/push.py`)
- `TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN` + `TELEGRAM_CHAT_ID`: enable Telegram push (both required; token is secret)
- `NTFY_TOPIC`: enable ntfy push to this topic (treat the topic name as a secret)
- `NTFY_SERVER` (default `https://ntfy.sh`): self-hosted ntfy server

### Live FX data (`src/ict_scanner.py`)
- `OANDA_API_KEY`: when set, the CLI live scanner fetches forex from OANDA (practice account) instead of the default fetcher (secret)

### Liquidity & IPO screen (`market_data/config.py`)
- `LIQUID_AVG_DAILY_VALUE_CR` (1.0) / `BORDERLINE_AVG_DAILY_VALUE_CR` (0.25): avg daily traded value (₹ cr) for liquid / borderline
- `LIQUID_MAX_ZERO_DAYS` (1) / `BORDERLINE_MAX_ZERO_DAYS` (4): max no-trade days in the lookback
- `LIQUIDITY_LOOKBACK_DAYS` (60): sessions used for the liquidity screen
- `MARKET_CAP_MIN_CR` (100.0), `FREE_FLOAT_MIN_PCT` (15.0), `BID_ASK_SPREAD_MAX_PCT` (2.0): liquidity screen thresholds
- `IPO_MIN_AVG_DAILY_VALUE_CR` (defaults to the borderline value, 0.25): min avg daily value for an IPO to be tracked
- `IPO_MIN_ACTIVE_RATIO` (0.5): min share of sessions traded since listing
- `IPO_MASTER_REFRESH_DAYS` (1): refresh interval for the NSE equity master (`EQUITY_L.csv`)
- `IPO_MAX_AGE_DAYS` (1095 = 3 years): how long after listing a symbol counts as an IPO
- Changing a liquidity/IPO threshold changes which symbols are screened or tracked
